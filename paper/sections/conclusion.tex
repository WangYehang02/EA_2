\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conc}

We presented a catalog-assisted, history-residual-guided re-ranking of PhaseNet
S-wave candidates that needs no picker fine-tuning.
On the Stage~3 event-disjoint 10k test, fixed re-ranking significantly improves
S F1@$0.5$ and reduces e2e P95 versus STEAD PhaseNet; a learned gate does not
outperform it.
Stage~4 provides only an underpowered directional audit.
We recommend the fixed score as the main method and advise against claiming
blind phase-picking SOTA or escalating to GNN complexity on this evidence.
