\item We do not provide a leakage-free, full-scale INSTANCE in-domain PhaseNet
trained under the same protocol as our STEAD baseline; existing finetuning
artifacts are debug-scale and did not beat STEAD under annotate evaluation.
A formal in-domain picker baseline remains a reviewer-facing gap, orthogonal to
catalog-assisted re-ranking.
\item Metrics and gains reported by regional Chinese pickers on DiTing
($\pm 0.6$\,s, $50$\,Hz) are not comparable to our INSTANCE protocol and must
not be used for ranking methods across papers.
\item Hierarchical multi-scale path residuals were explored on validation only;
even if promising on the development set, they are not part of the frozen
Stage~3 main results.
