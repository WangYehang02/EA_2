\textbf{Regional domain shift and regionalized pickers.}
Deep phase pickers trained in one region often degrade under changes in
subsurface structure, source characteristics, site response, noise, and
instrumentation~\citep{zhu2023ustcpickers}.
USTC-Pickers address this by training a China-wide PhaseNet-style model on
DiTing and then fine-tuning regional/provincial pickers, reporting that finer
customization yields diminishing returns relative to deployment complexity.
That line of work adapts \emph{waveform-network parameters}.
In contrast, the present paper freezes an external PhaseNet and re-ranks
existing S candidates using train-only historical path residuals.
The two strategies are complementary in principle, but our experiments do not
evaluate a joint system.
Importantly, DiTing evaluations at $\pm 0.6$\,s / $50$\,Hz are not numerically
comparable to our INSTANCE metrics at $\pm 0.1$/$0.5$\,s / $100$\,Hz.
