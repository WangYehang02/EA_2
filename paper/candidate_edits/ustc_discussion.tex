\paragraph{Complementarity with waveform adaptation.}
Regional transfer learning (e.g., USTC-Pickers) improves pickers by changing
network weights for a geographic domain~\citep{zhu2023ustcpickers}.
Our catalog-assisted re-ranking instead keeps PhaseNet fixed and injects a
train-only path-residual prior at the candidate-peak level.
These approaches target different failure modes and could be combined, but we
have not validated joint performance and therefore do not claim additive gains.

\paragraph{Diminishing returns of finer customization.}
USTC-Pickers discuss a trade-off between customization granularity, marginal
accuracy gains, and operational complexity.
We use that discussion only as qualitative motivation for preferring a simple
fixed score over heavier model stacks in our setting; it does \emph{not}
constitute evidence that graph neural networks are ineffective in general.

\paragraph{Distance-related S difficulty (exploratory).}
On our development/validation analyses, S travel-time and picking difficulty
tend to increase with epicentral distance, consistent in \emph{direction} with
reports on DiTing-based regional pickers.
This is not a cross-paper numerical claim and is not used to retune Stage~3/4
methods.
